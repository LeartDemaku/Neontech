Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common inquiries

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy on all our products. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you can return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. Items must be in their original packaging and in unused condition. Please note that shipping costs for returns are the responsibility of the customer unless the return is due to a defect or error on our part.

How long does shipping take?

Domestic shipping (within the US) typically takes 3-5 business days. International shipping varies by location but generally takes 7-14 business days. All orders are processed within 24-48 hours of purchase. You'll receive a tracking number via email once your order ships so you can monitor its progress.

Do your products come with a warranty?

Yes, all NEONTECH products come with a standard 1-year limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects and hardware malfunctions. Premium products include an extended 2-year warranty. Our warranty doesn't cover damage from accidents, misuse, or unauthorized modifications. To submit a warranty claim, please contact our support team with your order number and a description of the issue.

Can I cancel or modify my order?

Orders can be modified or canceled within 2 hours of placing them. After this window, we begin processing orders for shipment and can no longer make changes. To request a modification or cancellation, please contact our customer service team immediately with your order number.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to over 60 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by location. Please note that international orders may be subject to import duties, taxes, and customs clearance fees, which are the responsibility of the recipient. These charges are not included in our shipping fees or product prices.
